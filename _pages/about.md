Hello!

I'm Pushkaraj, an embedded and systems software engineer. Most recently I was a System Software Engineering Intern on the CUDA Driver Development team at NVIDIA, working with the CUDA Driver API, memory and interoperability patterns for low level GPU software. Before that, I was a Software Integration Intern at Tesla on the Cybercab program, delivering service tooling for fleet readiness of 2000+ RoboTaxi units, and a Systems Development Intern at AWS Annapurna Labs, where I owned a PXE boot migration that cut boot time from 9 minutes 30 seconds to 49 seconds across 100+ devices. Earlier, I was an Embedded Software Developer at Cummins, building BSP and firmware in C and C++ for engine controllers used in power generation products.

I earned my Master's in Computer Engineering from Texas A&M University (M.S. '26) and a Bachelor of Engineering in Electronics and Telecommunication from Savitribai Phule Pune University (B.E. '23). I also co-authored an IEEE paper, "Multi-modal Sensing and AI Perception of Humanoid Robot."

My technical interests include embedded software and firmware, systems software, boot and bring up, operating systems, hardware and software integration, and robotics. I enjoy working close to the hardware and debugging problems that cross the boundary between software and silicon.

I'm currently looking for full time roles in embedded, firmware and systems software. Feel free to reach out at [pushkaraj@tamu.edu](mailto:pushkaraj@tamu.edu) or connect with me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pushkarajsaid/).
