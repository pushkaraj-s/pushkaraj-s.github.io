This paper explores the synthesis of multi-modal sensing, IoT and AI within Robotic Process Automation (RPA), providing a framework for building humanoid service robots that can sense, perceive and assist people. It grew out of my final year project, an autonomous humanoid service robot built with Arduino and C.
